This is Coesite Operations' source glossary for **Huidu Guanli (灰度管理)**, the grayscale-management reading associated with Huawei's institutional practice and related Chinese thought. Its proposed enterprise concerns education and training; this repository currently contains **term explanations**, not proof of an installed teaching service, a trained client, or a verified account of current Huawei operations.

## Read the terms by their different functions

**[Dao](content/glossary/terms/dao.md)** names governing direction; **[Fa](content/glossary/terms/fa.md)** codified institutional rules; **[Shu](content/glossary/terms/shu.md)** practical methods; and **[Shi](content/glossary/terms/shi.md)** structural position or advantage. **[Wu wei](content/glossary/terms/wu-wei.md)** concerns qualified restraint from intervention, while **[Daoshi](content/glossary/terms/daoshi.md)** concerns the distinct mentoring role. Each entry owns its particular explanation and links related terms instead of reprinting the full hierarchy.
